Who may apply?
  • Filipino citizens aged 18–65.
  • Valid government-issued ID and basic information.
  • Active mobile number.
  • Regular income from employment, self-employment, business, or remittances.
Indicative loan features
  • Loan amounts: PHP 1,000 to PHP 50,000
  • Loan terms: Flexible tenors of 3, 6, 9, 12, or 24 months, with repayment options available on a weekly or monthly schedule to align with borrower cash flow
  • Nominal interest rate: 40% per annum (equivalent to approx. 3.33% per month / 0.11% per day)
  • All-in cost: all interest and applicable fees combined will never exceed the ceilings prescribed by the SEC
  • Total cost cap: you will never be charged interest, fees, charges and penalties exceeding 100% of the amount you borrowed, no matter how long the loan remains outstanding
What you receive before confirming a loan

Before you confirm any loan, you will receive a Loan Disclosure Statement showing the amount approved, the net amount you will receive, the interest rate (monthly and effective interest rate), the term, all fees and charges, any penalty for late payment, the repayment schedule and the total amount payable, in accordance with the Truth in Lending Act (R.A. No. 3765) and applicable SEC rules. No loan will be disbursed unless you have reviewed and expressly confirmed the final terms. Please study the terms and conditions in the Disclosure Statement before proceeding with any loan transaction.

About Us

Bidacash is the online lending platform of Luminous Lending Inc., a lending company duly registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) of the Philippines. We provide simple, transparent and accessible short-term personal loans to qualified Filipino borrowers — with every fee and charge clearly disclosed before you borrow.

Our Mission

To give everyday Filipinos — employees, workers, entrepreneurs and overseas Filipino workers — fair and convenient access to short-term credit, without hidden fees and without heavy paperwork.

Our Vision

To be a trusted digital lending platform supporting financial inclusion in the Philippines through transparent, responsible and technology-driven lending.

About Luminous Lending Inc.

Luminous Lending Inc. is a SEC-registered, compliance-focused lending company headquartered in Bonifacio Global City, Taguig, Metro Manila, Philippines, delivering responsible direct lending solutions to serve the domestic market. Our core business centers on customer-first direct lending services: in full alignment with Philippine financial regulatory frameworks, we do not engage in pawnbroking operations under Presidential Decree No. 114, nor operate as a financing company under Republic Act No. 8556.

Our lending approach

We design accessible personal lending products tailored to diverse customer needs, with flexible loan terms ranging from 3 to 24 months and convenient weekly or monthly repayment options to make borrowing manageable for every user. We uphold the highest standards of fair and transparent practice by strictly following all SEC mandates, including prohibitions on unfair debt collection tactics, mandatory clear disclosure for all lending advertisements, and standardized reporting for online lending platforms, to ensure every interaction with our customers is respectful, straightforward, and trustworthy.
